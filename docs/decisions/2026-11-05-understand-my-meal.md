# INSIGHT — Final Product Direction and Execution

**Owner-approved governing direction — 5 November 2026.** This replaces conflicting earlier product sequencing and orchestration instructions. Historical studies, outcomes, failed predictions and preservation records remain unchanged. Decision D governs quantitative engine selection and generalization, not whether a narrower evidence-aware learning experience may be built. The current Ionic/React application remains the implementation target for this slice; no new architecture or broad native expansion is authorized.

## 1. Our actual objective

**INSIGHT exists to make otherwise unconscious physiological processes more intelligible, beginning with meal-related insulin responses.**

This is inspired by the Jungian principle of bringing unconscious processes into conscious awareness. This is philosophical inspiration, not physiological validation.

We are not trying to make users minimize insulin, optimize a score, or judge their food choices.

**The user should understand something they previously didn't—and feel that they have gained that understanding.**

That is our central product outcome. The feeling must correspond to genuine learning. INSIGHT must not manufacture a sense of insight through misleading precision, speculative explanations, or impressive-looking numbers.

Success has two parts:

- **Actual understanding:** The person correctly understands what the evidence establishes, what is estimated, and what remains unknown.
- **Felt awareness:** The person experiences a meaningful discovery, recognizes something about their meal they hadn't considered, and feels more capable of exploring it themselves.

Neither substitutes for the other.

## 2. Design around curiosity and discovery

The primary experience is **Understand my meal**, not automatically Compare meals or Log my diet.

A person can enter or photograph a meal, correct ingredients and portions, and receive the strongest useful explanation the evidence supports. Existing unavailable photo-recognition paths must remain honestly unavailable rather than be simulated as working recognition; manual entry remains usable.

The experience answers in ordinary language:

- What do we know about this kind of meal?
- What might explain its insulin-response characteristics?
- What can and can't we infer about this particular meal?

The result should reveal something concrete, interesting and scientifically defensible. A person might discover that preparation changes responses in ways carbohydrate grams alone cannot describe, or that formulations with similar nutrition labels behaved differently in a published experiment. Such discoveries remain specific to the evidence, not invented explanations of personal physiology.

**Do not make every result a scientific report. Create clarity first and let curious users investigate the evidence.**

Comparison is optional exploration. Users may change an ingredient, serving or preparation and inspect the evidence for a difference. Saving and logging are optional; neither is required to receive value.

## 3. Make uncertainty informative

Keep four kinds of information distinct:

1. Direct research observations.
2. Estimates transferred from relevant references.
3. Predictions from models.
4. Information not yet established.

These describe the basis of a claim, not automatic confidence levels. Never disguise a population estimate as a personal insulin measurement.

Do not end at insufficient evidence when something useful is still known. Explain what the research establishes, what differs from the meal, and what information would be needed for a more specific answer. An unknown number can coexist with a useful discovery.

Unsupported predictions remain unavailable. Unknown responses are not zero. Never choose a convenient reference just to fill the screen. A related ingredient name does not establish a preparation match or justify quantitative transfer.

The current primary quantitative research endpoint remains population-average positive insulin area over 0–120 minutes under a specified protocol. Other endpoints remain explicitly labelled. Evidence of long-term health improvement is not a prerequisite for evaluating genuine learning; health outcomes are a separate claim.

## 4. Test whether awareness actually happens

Build a small usable experience in the existing application and observe unfamiliar users interacting with it. Evaluate:

1. Can they enter or correct a meal without assistance?
2. Can they articulate something specific they learned?
3. Can they distinguish a published observation from an estimate and from their own unmeasured insulin response?
4. Do they genuinely feel that they understand something better afterward?
5. Can they explore another question without being directed toward a supposedly healthier or lower-insulin choice?

Use observed behavior and users' own descriptions. Ask what surprised them, what became clearer and what remains uncertain. Record whether the explanation is scientifically correct.

High self-reported awareness accompanied by a false belief is not success. An accurate explanation that people find incomprehensible or meaningless is not a finished experience either.

Test a supported case and an insufficient-evidence case. Neither depends on daily logging or a universal prediction engine. Do not substitute model personas or software tests for observed human comprehension. When participant access is unavailable, complete the technical work and identify the unmeasured human outcomes honestly.

The objective is not maximizing engagement, compulsive checking or driving scores downward. It is making physiological evidence intelligible and worth understanding.

## 5. Model orchestration — explicit fallback

Astra owns scientific direction, architecture and acceptance. Opus 5.5 is the preferred implementation and research co-lead. Sol 6.1 independently verifies consequential calculations, code and findings.

**If Opus 5.5 is unavailable because of quota, tool failure, integration problems or provider restrictions, immediately use GLM 5.3 Flash through the configured available provider for permitted work.**

Do not freeze progress awaiting an Opus reset or repeatedly retry a blocked connection. Verify and record actual returned assistant identities and switching events. Never label GLM work as Opus work, or silently replace Flash with full GLM or another model. Lack of an accessible runtime must be disclosed rather than represented as a completed delegation. Keep completed contributions correctly attributed.

A genuine safety/policy refusal must be preserved. Do not route the prohibited operation to another provider to circumvent it. Continue unaffected permitted work. Opus may resume when available without creating another dependency.

## 6. Finish a product capability

The next deliverable is **one complete meal-understanding experience in the existing INSIGHT application**.

Preserve the functioning meal editor, portion correction, local storage, provenance and historical evidence. Do not restore traffic-light food judgments or the unsupported chronic insulin-resistance pressure score. Do not begin broad Android expansion, a new architecture or an open-ended literature review.

Use the accumulated research to demonstrate a meaningful supported observation and an honest unknown. Judge the result on scientific fidelity, comprehensibility, felt awareness and discovery, user agency, realistic input availability and clear limitations. Then decide to retain, improve, simplify or narrow it.

Future research must answer a question that materially changes what users can understand or what INSIGHT can responsibly display. The team has freedom over methods and implementation; the deliverable is fixed.

## 7. Final standard

> I understand something about what happens when I eat this that I didn't understand before. I know why the app says it, what it doesn't know, and I can explore it further without being told what I should choose.

Continue to a working product demonstration and evidence-based user-journey assessment—not another indefinite scientific research loop.
