# Two separately collected 35 g reference drinks

These are cross-study absolute endpoints. Both used a 300 mL plain water vehicle and plasma insulin iAUC above fasting through 120 minutes. Neither study gave both sugar forms. Their cohorts and assays differ, overlap is unverified, and the papers give no same-cohort pure-glucose references. Use each arm as an absolute descriptive stress test only. The dose convention is nominal dry mass, rather than matched molar yield after hydrolysis.

| Source and reference | Sample | Positive iAUC mean | SD | Fasting mean (SD) | Plasma assay |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Törrönen et al., AJCN 2012, Table 3, 35 g freshly added intact sucrose | 20 healthy women | 1373 | 449 | 5.3 (1.5) | Siemens ADVIA Centaur Insulin IRI |
| Törrönen et al., J Nutr Sci, 2017, Table 3, 17.5 g glucose plus 17.5 g fructose | 18 healthy adults, 13 women and 5 men | 1089 | 492 | 5.0 (2.0) | DiaSorin Liaison Insulin |

Units for areas are mU·min/L, numerically equivalent to µU·min/mL. Fasting concentrations are mU/L. The 2017 authors specify a pmol/L per mU/L factor of 6.945. Applying this factor to 2012 cannot establish calibration across the two assay platforms. Both studies sampled fasting and 15, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120 min from starting the drink and used GraphPad with area below fasting ignored. The 2017 study reported a 10–12 h fast. The 2017 study excluded one individual from glucose analysis only. Its insulin sample was all 18.
