## Source and error boundaries

- **Lee.** Two official Library and Archives Canada routes supplied byte-identical PDF copies, SHA-256 `7542242f2ede3d993b2f612e3a5c77b05f1844a8eeb26a5bd5ab4a2a467501ac`. Both the coordinator and Sol inspected the original insulin row labels, means, SEMs and starred free-mixture footnote. The source’s randomized blocks used ascending or descending dose order within blocks. Conditions shared participants and a subject-specific water or tea vehicle. The 50 g fructose response is from seven participants. The remaining admitted arms are from eight. Published normalized indices based on another reference are not raw native means. [Primary thesis](https://www.collectionscanada.ca/obj/s4/f2/dsk2/ftp04/mq28791.pdf)
- **External reach.** Grüneis was a healthy male crossover and provides participant-positive two-hour evidence. Jameel’s 50 g, n=14 crossover labels its two insulin panels as percent changes. Integrating percent changes at 0, 30, 60 and 120 min gives a different result from the printed area bars. Its normalization remains unresolved. We neither convert those results into concentration units nor choose one according to which favors the model. [Grüneis](https://doi.org/10.1002/mnfr.202000472) · [Jameel](https://doi.org/10.1186/1476-511X-13-195)
- **Free mixture.** The protocol was 1 g/kg total carbohydrate in active adults. The 1:1 mixture contained 0.5 g/kg of each. Actual fasting concentrations were used. The acquisition investigator isolated Figure 1C by stacking PDF image objects 105 and 106 (1,228 × 788 pixels each), cropping (0, 530, 610, 1040) and resizing to 915 × 765. Source-based reading scenarios bracket the 15-minute overlap, axis registration and rounded fasting values. Sol’s broader ±0.5 mU/L point scenarios can reverse even the two-contrast average ranking in a joint extreme scenario. The central negative sucralose signed area stays visible. [Eckstein](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu13114095) · [Coordinate method](support/extract_mixture.py)
- **Muffins.** The breakfasts comprised three muffins and a near-matched high fat load. Cream differed between recipes. Optional milk in tea or coffee was held consistent within participants. Composition was estimated with FoodWorks. The three published starch-plus-sugar totals are used as effective carbohydrate because they equal the reported totals to the printed precision. We do not infer another fibre subtraction. Figure 3 yields signed group-curve integrals. Figure 4 provides the reported four-hour iAUC with an unspecified below-baseline convention. The authors found a fructose/sucrose four-hour difference, while the sucralose/sucrose comparison was not distinguished. The endpoint and model-error hypotheses differ. Sol's alternative early coordinates gave F/S about 0.758 and U/S about 0.900, versus the coordinator’s 0.718 and 0.895. The conclusions use point forecasts, with independent reading limits and no invented confidence interval. [Gallagher](https://doi.org/10.3945/ajcn.115.129866) · [Recipe and reading receipt](support/GALLAGHER.md)
- **Prior baking evidence.** The potato cookie reports positive two-hour serum area and the maize scone reports two-hour plasma iAUC without explicit clipping. Sugar identity was unavailable from those tables. The interval in the label audit is over unknown food inputs, not an uncertainty interval on insulin response. [Cookie](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu9030237) · [Scone](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu10020129)
