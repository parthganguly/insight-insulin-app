Independent direct squared-loss computation matches `native/evaluate.py` MSE differences, SEs and Welch degrees of freedom for all56 CPF-vs-C/constant comparisons it reports. Portable `secondary_verify.py` calculates72 across all9 portable input settings (adds actual-recipe egg bounds). Run `verify.py` first.

For fixed forecasts a,b, mean loss difference is mean(a²-b²)+sum[2(b-a)y/6]. Sampling variance under independent arm estimates is sum[(2(b-a)SEM/6)²]. Shared outcome squared terms cancel exactly. The shape version centers each prediction vector across six arms; centered outcome or uncentered outcome gives identical difference because the weights sum to zero. This checks relative shape after removing the common mean, and is a different, posthoc objective from frozen MAE/log-ratioMAE. It does not estimate a target anchor used in predictions.

Variance calculation is conditional on fixed forecasts, historical fit, insulin conversion and serving/dose estimates. Mean-body-mass/food-mass inputs are estimated in the same trial; their uncertainty and covariance with insulin outcomes are ignored. Individual serving proportionality makes affine prediction at the true mean input equal mean prediction, but rounded printed means/compositions are still estimates. Training coefficient uncertainty, assay conversion uncertainty, graph-reading uncertainty, source endpoint inconsistency and protocol transport uncertainty are excluded. GraphSEM values are approximate. WelchSatterthwaite df is a finite-sample approximation relying on independent normal-arm mean/variance behavior, not an exact distribution-free guarantee. The same arms recur across comparisons, which are dependent.
