# Decisions, exposure and reproducibility

- **Parent closed.** PR #143 at `12d3042e0461d2593f4aa83817d36dc5818f8814`, [posted independent review](https://github.com/parthganguly/insight-insulin-app/pull/143#pullrequestreview-5783375427). The accepted Linux replay and independent reconstruction were not reopened. The favorable equal-grams comparison for positive trehalose areas did not select 0.420 over all other reduction forecasts. The graded polymer result, prior meal and protein exceptions, sucrose failure and calibration/portion limits remain recorded at that commit.
- **What was known.** [Initial plan](PLAN.md) and its SHA timestamp precede review of the new IGT numerical areas by the coordinator. Study identity and headline direction were known; Yoshizane, Holub and one Maastricht donor series had been seen. The other donor and target came from published archives, not a prospectively conducted trial. The primary objective was discrimination between the two fractional substitutions, separately by protocol. The scientific endpoint is participant-positive 0–120; signed two-hour group curves were the planned evaluable support. The three-hour author-reported iAUCs are a separate operator with an unspecified clipping convention.
- **Sources reconciled.** The [authors’ own joint abstract, S134, T3:PS.12](https://www.nature.com/articles/ijo200847.pdf) identifies one healthy overweight donor cohort with four sugars. Both 2009 companion papers are needed for the curves; they are not two independent groups. The later IGT cohort is separately described, but participant identities across studies are unavailable. Target and donor baseline samples were nominally −5 minutes; carrying to zero and interpolating to zero are provided. Lunch was 25% of individualized total lunch energy from the study sugar, and used the same morning assignment. No fixed 75 g lunch or meal-only causal interpretation.
- **One real source conflict.** The two donor articles print exactly the same afternoon means 10,693/14,634 for the different pairs. Even with −5 to +180 included, absolute, *unsubtracted* bounds from the isomaltulose/sucrose curves are 10,158/11,361. Positive clipping cannot reconcile those numbers under matching complete records and nonnegative baselines. Who or what accounts for the discrepancy is unknown. Source Table 1 is recorded as printed; its lunch equality is not accepted as a forecast. The primary independently derived signed integrals remain usable. Fasting reported three-hour D transferred almost exactly (.782 vs .785) but the two underlying ratios were both underpredicted; a useful relative result, not accurate absolute calibration.

Opus implemented the comparisons and portable replay, questioned the duplicated tables, and preserved its original three-hour implementation. On correction it recovered the planned signed two-hour and secondary trajectory scores, cross-checked the donor and target extractions, and withdrew an unsupported claim about the direction of the SE error. See [corrected implementation](opus_pair/REPORT.md) and [execution](opus_pair/EXECUTION.json); the earlier version remains historical. Some donor-derived forecasts were shared; verification did not constitute a blinded clinical replication. The first optional image check lacked four files; the later six-image check passed. The final source audit repeats the six-image check plus PDF checks.
- **What changed after looking.** The three nested windows were nominated in the initial plan. Optimizing equal-window log error relative to the mathematically best constant on D, the directly compared T/I ratio, the finite-sample bounds, and the joint reading stresses are subsequent diagnostics. In `timecourse.py` the all-upper and all-lower D values are simultaneously attainable at a given radius: move every post-dose T/S ordinate up and its baseline down, reverse for G/I (or conversely). They are not samples from a distribution. Error ranges describe graph readings, not uncertainty in the n=10 adults.
- **Next evaluation boundary.** [External protocol](SHAPE_PLAN.md) and [Holub comparator amendment](SHAPE_COMPARATORS.md) were fingerprinted before the coordinator saw the 1985 figure outcomes. Kawai was chosen for named drinks, fasting crossover and sampling design; no Kawai response was used to fit or select the transferred coefficients. The single-hour fixed comparator was added after seeing these results. The earlier Suraphad, Holub and Yoshizane panels are labelled development. Holub is never scored as an external evaluation of itself. The published 1989 diabetes citation is not a verified numerical panel; no values or precision were imputed. [Acquisition and second reader](kawai/README.md).
- **Scale and limitations.** An error on an area *ratio level* is not error in a native area. Zero crossings on interpolated group curves need not be participant crossing times. There is no paired uncertainty for the change in all four arms or the nested-window ratio-of-ratios; the Kawai published test refers to the sum of increments. Population-average prediction, clinical generalization, personal scoring, and independent portion scaling have not been validated. No app change or outreach.
- **Artifacts.** Analysis inputs are aggregate graph coordinates, factual transcriptions, documented source links and derived forecasts. Original publisher assets, connection/session records and private correspondence are excluded. The original Sol laboratory run also inventoried locally present source assets; the offline runner compares its scientific calculations, not a reconstruction of that local filesystem. The optional audit separately authenticates the exact original publications and recovers all 56 donor coordinates. The older `opus_pair/MODEL_RECEIPT.json` is an implementer self-report; [participation.json](participation.json) is based on the live Claude Code route and actual returned assistant message identity. Historical notes retain their contemporaneous qualifications where the reconciliation supersedes them.
