# Using the evaluated cohort's own reference profile

The slow carbohydrate-first arm in Imai's crossover supplies a direct check of the 14-minute translation. Using this cohort's reference curve gives a predicted carbohydrate-last/first ratio of **0.9065** when integrating the translated piecewise-linear curve and **0.9233** when resampling it on the original 0/30/60/120-minute grid first. The ratio of published arm mean insulin areas is **0.7404**. Both forecasts move toward the observed mean from 1. The simpler flat-window forecast of 106/120 = 0.8833 is closer. This calculation requires measured insulin in the evaluation cohort. [Imai et al., Figure 3B, Table 2 and results text](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu15051174)

The authors report slow carbohydrate-first mean insulin concentrations of 83.6 and 58.2 µIU/mL at 30 and 60 minutes. We read the baseline and terminal values from the original 3974 × 2594 pixel graph. With the origin at the upper left, the y coordinates for 0 and 100 µIU/mL were 1153.5 and 212.5. The baseline marker is occluded. Extending the visible first segment from the centers at (2540,999.5), (2570,931.5), (2600,863.5) and (2620,818.5) back to x = 2499 gave y = 1093. The terminal open black marker is centered near (3780,749). These readings give about 6.43 and 42.99 µIU/mL. No treatment area was used to obtain the concentrations. [Original publisher figure](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/nutrients/nutrients-15-01174/article_deploy/html/images/nutrients-15-01174-g003.png)

The first reader allowed y = 1152–1155 and 212–213 for the two axis ticks, 1082–1104 for baseline, and 744–754 at 120 minutes. Across the 16 combinations of those endpoints, the two forecast ranges are 0.9048–0.9084 and 0.9216–0.9251. They describe finite graph-reading scenarios. Paired sampling uncertainty and the unsampled curve between blood draws are separate. The central reference integral is 5741.5, compared with the reported mean participant area of 5702. Averaging and positive clipping need not commute.
