## Evidence boundaries

D is the available carbohydrate reported for an actual serving. Bar and cookie F are serving total dietary fibre. They include fibre from other ingredients, and their values can depend on formulation software and analytical method. In the original wheat bar paper, 27.2 g represents the ingredient and about 14 g finished RS is the authors’ conditional estimate. In the potato-bar paper Table 2 ingredient columns sum near 100 despite the gram label and varying Table 1 servings. We used Table 1 as eaten. Cookie servings had equal printed total carbohydrate, 36.84 g, while their available carbohydrate differed by 23.57 g. The scones used a different, acid-hydrolysed and heat-treated maize ingredient and supplied 42.8 versus 46.4 g total carbohydrate. Source names alone cannot harmonize these inputs. [Numerical source extract](source_facts.json)

Gourineni et al. counted area above the premeal value, assigning the −5-minute sample to zero. Stewart and Zimmer explicitly used only positive incremental areas and reported untransformed arithmetic summaries. Its Table 3 also places an hour in the peak-concentration unit label, so we checked the time scale against original Figure 2c. The coordinator's signed group-curve estimates were about 13,563 and 6,731 pmol·min/L. Sol independently read about 13,328 and 6,923. The table’s participant-positive results convert to 13,740 and 7,440 by multiplying hours by 60. Both readings support that scale, with mean-curve integration and mean person-level positive area kept separate. The scone paper used a −15-minute fasting baseline and reported plasma trapezoidal iAUC. Its citation to glycaemic-index methods supports, but does not establish, the clipping convention for insulin. Wheat donor, cookie and scone also differ in assay or specimen documentation. [Cookie Figure 2 and methods](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu9030237) · [Independent reading](sol/cookie_curve_scale.json) · [Scone methods](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu10020129)

Use the four article URLs, PMCID, source table identifiers and raw XML hashes in `source_facts.json` to locate the original inputs. The optional checker reads the original Europe PMC JATS files named by PMCID. The coordinator separately checked the portion means and SEMs against the plasma-insulin rows of Table 2 in the retained WB1996 publisher extraction. Its SHA-256 is in `wb_portions.json`. Source papers, private transport logs and individual records are outside the handoff. The published trials involved industry funding or investigator interests. Missing within-participant covariances limit uncertainty on the model rankings.
