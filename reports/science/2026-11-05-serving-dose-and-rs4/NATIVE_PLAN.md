# Externally anchored native-area test

Exposure: the cold-pressed potato means and baked potato cookie results have now been inspected, including both serving tables. On the cookie, the D-only ratio already falls below the observed area ratio. Negative attenuation of D with fibre can only worsen that ratio. A simple food-energy comparator is relevant because the foods retain similar fat and protein when starch is replaced. Test fixed compositions rather than choose another fibre coefficient. A priori for this extension select Stewart et al. 2018 baked scone (10.3390/nu10020129, PMC5852705) as a separate evaluation. Search snippets have exposed that insulin area falls and a peak change; the coordinator has not inspected its numerical native insulin areas, grams or ratio. The independent external target uses another RS4 ingredient and another recipe. This is a sequential exposed-development test with separately evaluated numerical outcomes, not a validation of a production model.

Use the 2017 control cookie's mean native positive 0–120 serum insulin area as the sole development scale. Multiply it by the target serving score divided by the training control's serving score. Never use evaluation-cohort insulin for this scale. Compare constant, D (published available carbohydrate), D+P (equal gram weights), and conventional energy proxy 4D+4P+9fat. The energy proxy is a fixed transformation of three label inputs and is not a physiological conversion. Compare printed kcal separately if both studies supply them. Predict both native levels in pmol·min/L, their absolute decrement and their ratio. Primary native criterion is two-arm mean absolute error; comparative criterion is absolute log error of the RS4/control ratio. Score independently and retain failures. An additive protein/fat term can moderate an exaggerated carbohydrate-ratio change because protein/fat remain in the reformulated food; do not tune weights to this or prior cookie means. For cross-recipe stress, separately use the cold-pressed control to anchor the same measures where units and definitions support it. State if cohort, plasma/serum, assay, sampling, missingness or unit convention limits transfer; any fixed unit convention must be based on the source methods or shown only conditionally. Preserve within-study unitless contrasts if native conversion is unavailable. Do not pool studies. No new validated native claim follows from one favourable meal ratio.
