# Reference selection and transfer rules
Decision: when moving out of the originating laboratory, can the existing empirical reference give source contrasts, normalized levels and coverage on protein drinks, and can a measured composition neighborhood supply missing preparations? This is exposed development. Search results and original Acheson article already disclosed all three two-hour means and qualitative rankings, the Thondre and forest means were previously exposed. No coefficients are fitted to those outcomes. Rules below precede computation of distances, numerical forecasts and panel errors. Enumerate all testable pairs, do not change success metrics.
- Primary Acheson 2011: compare whey, soy and casein energy-matched breakfasts. Casein has no literally matching Claessens ch2 reference. Strict same formulation/product coverage unavailable. Coarser species/source family matches W and S only; no casein imputation. Ratios are native reported two-hour IAUC means, not participant-normalized indices. Treat source carbohydrate alone and target glucose as the matched carbohydrate reference in each experiment. Show also the higher-energy carbohydrate meal as an alternative denominator, not interchangeable with glucose. Compare composition across protein sources; use all 16 shared AA fields excluding Asp/Asx and Glu/Glx due the ambiguous amide definition. Close each vector to the shared total, total variation = half L1. Primary profile rule = closest of six external profiles, transfer its entire response / carbohydrate-alone. Retain all distances and selection margins; ties use average response. Evaluate conditional on profile availability and report every 3-way pair versus tie; assess one source-labeled pair separately. NN extrapolations have no laboratory-certified support radius; also show removing nearest source and 18-field pooled-amide assumption. Include fixed leucine substitution slope if gram basis can be justified; otherwise conditional only. Distinguish grams of product from grams of protein.
- Empirical ratio level R and optional dose mapping 1+(R-1)d where d=(P:C)_target/(P:C)_source, gram basis and equal carbohydrate/protein energy factors for Acheson. No claim of proportional law. Ratios across two meals divide the predictions, so reference cancels. Simple references = tie/no change, energy where known, external mean of all six ratios for source-blind levels. All error in natural absolute logs; average over 3 formulation pairs or 3 ratios to glucose separately, and common coverage for named source. Do not call an ordering or a near point estimate absolute validation.
- Broader falsification: published Wolever 120-minute forest, compare the same two unfitted R and 1+(R-1)d rules in every external first-author group with exact literal names among six ch2 sources. Exclude Claessens source data, preserve reported and calculated endpoints separately, no deduping by looking at response. For comparable exactly-equal protein/carbohydrate-dose source pairs WITHIN one author/year with shared control/protocol documented, report contrasts separately; unresolved linkage unavailable. No blind-validation claim. Each author receives same total weight on level panel. Compare no change and source-blind incremental reference (use mean R of six, same d); retain favorable exceptions, missing and unrepresented source labels. Use previously fixed held-author constant/dose as stronger baselines where reference panel permits, no new fitting to reported performance. Level is relative to control. No mixing native units, duration or surrogate outcomes.
- Pure-form comparisons (Opus/Astra) have independent local freeze and acquisition records. Never pool these pure-protein dose and hydrolysis comparisons into the mixed-meal accuracy figure. Deciding rule throughout: do broader measurements upgrade coverage and improve on simpler predictions on the same offered rows? Report abstentions and identify actual information required.
