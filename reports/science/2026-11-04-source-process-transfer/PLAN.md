# Frozen transfer tests: can a source-specific preparation response travel?

We want to decide whether INSIGHT should carry a coefficient for each food source across different operations or learn a response conditional on the actual process and a measured/obtainable starch property. Two quantitative tests use independent cohorts. The evidence routes were chosen after the published Bornet reversal and selection of earlier references. Granfeldt 2000's qualitative oat/barley difference, some indexed values and the rice review's Larsen estimates have already been exposed. No arm means acquired after this freeze will be called untouched prospective validation. No target insulin enters any primary prediction or parameter.

## Thickness transfer (Granfeldt 2000)

Use actual matched pretreatment comparisons: roasted 1 mm oats vs roasted 0.5 mm oats; steamed 1 mm barley vs steamed 0.5 mm barley, if both original arm means available. Oats and barley have different pretreatments; distinguish a grain × rolling operation contrast conditional on protocols from a pure grain interaction. All servings must have documented comparable doses and actual cooking/serving information; report separate reference if needed. Primary endpoint is each printed arithmetic mean positive/incremental 120-min insulin area. Separate 90-min, normalized index and group-curve results; record clipping/denominator and inability to infer paired errors. Compute thick/thin ratio for each grain, and their ratio (oat ratio divided by barley ratio). Score absolute log error separately on each ratio and on the change in contrast. Never use significance as equality.

Equality/common geometry predicts double ratio 1; pure inverse thickness predicts 0.5 for both under the area-per-unit-mass approximation, distinct from digestion; common grain kernel/fine reference is the median of Heaton wheat/maize/oat, predicted the same for both in the absence of source-specific learning. Transport the Heaton oat factor 1111/1323 and two Granfeldt 1994 covered barley kernel/flour factors 5.8/11.0 and 5.2/10.2. Use geometric mean of the two barley ratios as primary, individual alternatives and source-specific missing-person envelopes as sensitivities. Predict thick/thin using each donor as-is; any common severity power k>0 softening the operation preserves their ordering. No selection of k from the flake outcomes. Declare other reasonable form-based hypotheses and uncertainty; numeric comparison is conditional on transport from whole kernels versus flour to thickness, not interchangeable data. Inspect DSC sample conditioning, linkage, water/oil and normalization. Record measurements that do not vary within the tested pairs or cannot score them as unavailable or tie according to actual inputs. 

## Amylose and preparation

Develop a food-input model on Bornet1989's exposed raw suspensions and cooked-and-cooled gels. Use ONLY three matched botanical sources with measured Table 1 amylose (wheat, cassava and smooth pea), equal 35 g starch, and Table 5 printed 180-min incremental insulin means. Fit log area vs log amylopectin fraction on a separate intercept/slope for each process. Equal weight per source; train each slope once. Frozen independent-cohort candidate predicts meal ratios via ratios of scores proportional to (1-amylose fraction)^b for each declared preparation. Raw and heated states are distinct. Compare against equality, amylopectin mass with unit exponent, a shared slope estimated after centering within raw/gel groups (common-state model), and fully cooked slope. Industrial training excluded because source and preparation differ (mung noodles vs pea). No estimate of absolute insulin units from these ratio forecasts. State small-n confounding, rank nonmonotonicity, raw magnitudes, and sensitivity to dropping one training source (two sources identify a slope but not a structural law).

Test external cooked samples with original known amylose definition/portion and native insulin group endpoint. Prefer independently collected graded composition within a *fixed* cooked recipe (Behall et al. graded maize starch bread or arroz), and/or rice varieties with matched or separate milling state. For 50g fixed starch contrast nutritional mass and recipe adequate only when source-linked. Published available carbohydrate, nominal starch blend %, actual flour/loaf amylose % are not interchangeable. No classification of fresh cooked rice/bread as identical to isolated starch cooled 24h; the candidate relies on broad heat-state transport. Score all eligible arms vs prespecified common comparator (lowest-amylose meal within batch/process; retain adjacent graded contrasts and compare high/low in second view). One row per recipe; do not fit or choose a new threshold from evaluation means. If original gives normalized insulin index only, separate branch, not native. If only prints total AUC or peaks, separate from true incremental 120/180. Common-source/heating multipliers may still predict useful levels despite failing to capture the previous ordering.

## Reference dependence

Prior affine f(h)=1+lambda(h-1) was evaluated on oriented pairs. f(a/b) is generally different from f(a/c)/f(b/c). Preserve the parent forecasts. A common-reference score model must choose an actual anchor and unit; design separate experimental scores if evidence supports one. No equivalence between a proportional assay change and a validated absolute insulin calibration.

## Acquisition

Primary publicly available trials only for quantitative outcomes. Retain original evidence and exact attrition/ingredients. Miller/Pang/Bramall, Larsen and Bangladesh rice are candidates; for all comparisons use their documented insulin data, retain fail and absence results. Previously read qualifying abstracts, a few numerical summaries from search, accepted prior results and other source exposure are acknowledged. No outreach.
