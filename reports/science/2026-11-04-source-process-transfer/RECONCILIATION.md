## Admission and outcome exposure

- Granfeldt 2000 was already an acquisition lead. The qualitative oat/barley result, some indexed barley outcomes and study design were known when the flake prediction was frozen. Sol separately recovered the publisher PDF text and tables. The primary roasted oat and steamed barley contrasts each hold pretreatment constant within grain. Their between-grain comparison does not hold pretreatment constant. Shared authors, recruitment site and partial similarities in participant descriptions with the 1994 donor prevent assuming disjoint recruitment. The bread reference two-hour mean happens to be 18.0 in both papers, but SEMs, 90-minute reference values and glucose summaries differ. [Flake source](https://doi.org/10.1093/jn/130.9.2207)
- The Bornet fit used exposed published three-hour area means. The frozen exponents, raw source values, serving definition and all leave-one-source fits are retained. Six arm means do not identify a causal effect of amylose; the fitted model was allowed to fail outside the training materials. There were no test-study insulin levels in the food forecasts. [Main protocol](PLAN.md) · [Prior source](https://doi.org/10.1093/ajcn/50.2.315)
- The 2019 wheat trial was selected for an equal-weight 2×2 source-by-flour comparison. The general direction and the reported broad reduction were known before quantitative extraction. Amylose refers to wheat starch, not percentage of the bread's wet serving. The [serving extension](WHEAT_PLAN.md) fixed starch dose handling before the coordinator inspected arm-specific responses. The Table 3 arithmetic/prose conflict, graph-derived arm areas, positive-area bounds and two-pool necessary conditions are subsequent checks. None uses new outcomes as training data. The Figure 2 coordinate ranges describe reading variation; the individual area bounds describe all fixed-sample linear trajectories on that grid consistent with nonnegative concentrations. Neither gives population uncertainty.
- Hoebler was selected because a matched food assay, a preparation contrast and separately collected human outcomes were jointly available. The white-bread-referenced indices were exposed before the [offset score protocol](ASSAY_PLAN.md). Numerical markers and signed two-hour areas were acquired afterward. Its normalized indices are averages of participant-specific ratios; dividing two indices is not a ratio of native mean positive areas. The exact sampling convention and absolute membership at every late point could not be independently verified. We report the Figure 1B signed area through 120, interpolating between its 105- and 140-minute markers. Table 1's available starch exceeds reported total starch for spaghetti, and the assay caption uses glucose-equivalent units. The alternative 0.9 conversion and ±2-pixel clinical reading envelopes were retained. [Hoebler original](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.ejcn.1600718)
- Costabile was chosen after the favorable 2019 starch-minus-RS comparison specifically to try to overturn it. The paper's title and study description had exposed a later-lunch effect; the new [first-meal plan](REPLICATION_PLAN.md) did not switch to that endpoint. The paper did not publish the numerical total starch measured in each served bread. Its 70/85 designations are flour substitution percentages rather than amylose concentrations. It enrolled adults with obesity, provided jam and matched available carbohydrates, unlike the equal-weight bread trial. Do not equate doses or infer starch contents from the insulin outcome. [Primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tjnut.2022.10.007)
- No single pooled loss across studies was used. Source ratios and preparation contrasts are primary. Published marginal positive IAUC, graph-derived signed areas, cohort-normalized indices and differently timed areas are labeled separately. Some illustrations multiply a prediction by a known evaluation-study control area to place it on a scale; this is explicit measured-control calibration. Only ratios are forecast without evaluation-study insulin.

## Other rice evidence and exclusions

Astra's original-figure check of Ito's crossover (15 with venous insulin) gave brown/white rice ratios of 0.987 at 60 and 0.851 at 120 minutes. A prior Malaysian same-variety brown/polished 180-minute insulin-index ratio was 39/63 = 0.619. Transferring an external brown/white early-hydrolysis ratio predicted 0.571; applying the earlier power to that ratio gave 0.866, closer for the two-hour Japanese comparison. Equality was closer for the first hour. The rice source and cookers/water differ across studies. In a different population of nine adults with type 2 diabetes, Larsen's same-batch BR16 traditional and pressure parboiled / untreated ratios were 1.016 and 0.999 on the separate three-hour endpoint. These observations support keeping simple references and protocol-specific exceptions in the comparison. [Ito](https://doi.org/10.2152/jmi.52.159) · [Karupaiah](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21480266/) · [Rice assay](https://doi.org/10.1093/ajcn/34.10.1991) · [Larsen 2000](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.ejcn.1600969) · [Rice calculations](rice_results.json)

A separate Bangladesh report prints non-parboiled BR16 1078 ± 1268 versus parboiled 12811 ± 1505 (mean ± SD, n=17), alongside a claim of no detected difference. Even the maximum possible paired SD of 2773 cannot reconcile those numbers and that claim under the described paired analysis. Some glucose means in this table coincide with values attributed by a review to Larsen 1996; several insulin means are close and the untreated value differs. The primary Bangladesh table and a secondary transcription of the other report do not resolve which values or populations were intended. We left the printed values intact and did not count independent replications or replace 1078 with 11087. The Australian three-variety Miller panel likewise lacks primary confirmation of all numerical arm cells and the index reference; it was not used for the principal verdict. No clarification was sent. [Parvin original table](https://banglajol.info/index.php/IMCJ/article/download/2925/2426) · [Larsen 1996 primary design](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8617187/) · [Comparison of literature reports](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007114515001841) · [Miller primary abstract](https://doi.org/10.1093/ajcn/56.6.1034)
