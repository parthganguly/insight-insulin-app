The 2019 four-bread analysis has now been scored: the reported high/conventional insulin ratio at 180 minutes is 0.658 and the ratio of labeled starch less resistant starch is 0.660. This close agreement is outcome-exposed and motivated this extension. Freeze the comparator before quantitative inspection of Costabile et al. 2023 breakfast bread outcomes. This related lead was identified via its qualitative title (which emphasizes the second meal), before acquiring the first-meal insulin magnitudes. No new coefficient. Clearly distinguish previously encountered 2023 abstract/protocol from unseen numbers.

Do not subtract RS twice or infer an ingredient amylose fraction from 70%/85% flour substitution.
