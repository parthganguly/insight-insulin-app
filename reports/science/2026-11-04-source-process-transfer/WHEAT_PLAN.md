# Equal-weight breads from high- and conventional-amylose wheat

Belobrajdic et al. 2019 offers a 2 by 2 factorial: HAW/LAW and wholemeal/refined in one crossover of twenty people, 121-g portions. The abstract's broad 24–30% reduction and nonsignificant processing statement were exposed at selection; the coordinator has not seen arm-specific numeric insulin outcomes or scored predictions. Independently sourced trial, conditional transport with previously exposed qualitative result. Primary contrasts: high/standard in each milling form, and wholemeal/refined separately within each source. Score ratio of arithmetic mean incremental insulin areas at original endpoint, signed 0–120 curve sensitivity if recoverable, and double ratio for interaction. Include all four arms. Do not convert a P-value to equality. Assess real meal response levels separately from contrasts and a measured-control benchmark; no target insulin level anchors food-only forecasts.

The Bornet per-meal model was fitted at equal starch dose. For the as-served primary high/low forecast compare the fixed heat exponent applied to each served starch amount: S=grams total starch × (1−amylose fraction)^b. Includes the simple b=1 amylopectin-dose baseline, separately total starch, available carbohydrate/digestible starch (if source-specific data) and equal-serving/no change. Multiplying a fixed-dose meal score linearly by measured starch dose is an extra, unvalidated portion assumption; retain the fixed-dose ratios unmodified as a composition-only sensitivity. Any use of per-100g values must use consistent moisture/serving basis (all 121g); actual measured available carbohydrate with resistant starch already excluded must not have it subtracted again. Use actual recipe/starch sources, macronutrient differences and reported range of amylose fractions; distinguish measured or manufacturer variety characteristic and meal total. Formula does not automatically apply to unmeasured recipe amylose. Record assay availability. Sol should independently check all consequential calculations. No learning from these target means. No invented absolute levels or curve digitization without original. This family of trials can be informative even if no sign reversal occurs.
