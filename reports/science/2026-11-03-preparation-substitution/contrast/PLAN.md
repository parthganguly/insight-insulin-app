# Common full-replacement condition and a reciprocal test

At full exchange, replacing all available carbohydrate from either plain rice or plain potato with the same lentil preparation and the same carbohydrate dose yields one lentil condition. For response A and lentil L, (L/P)/(L/R)=R/P. The predictions therefore imply a rice/potato response ratio even when the target paper did not serve lentils. This checks a necessary consequence under shared preparation, background, dose, eating protocol and endpoint. A favorable result does not establish individual L/R or L/P predictions or the fraction rule for mixtures. With incomplete exchange, the two mixtures are not identical; do not cancel them.
