## Algebra to verify once forecast is shared
For log-response slope beta and baseline proportion f0 and target f1, explicit transport law is logR(f1)=logR(f0)*f1/f0. This is a model assumption, not implied by equal energy or by FII definition. Alternative additive equalC model: R(f)=1-f+f*r, with r the per-availableC response ratio. A half-replacement observation implies r=2Rhalf-1 only under additive linearity; a negative inferred r diagnoses incompatibility with this simple physical model. Neither law should be silently selected based on held-out outcomes.
