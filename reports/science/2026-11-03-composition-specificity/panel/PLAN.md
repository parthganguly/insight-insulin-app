Assess high→medium and high→low vs medium→low, unweighted mean abs log error; these three are dependent. Use the authors' reported 0–120 insulin-AUC estimates (arbitrary units) only on their reported scale. Do not infer individual-positive or linear concentration AUC from log-scale geometric mean markers. Preserve AUC casein results, differing sample membership, designation and pairwise inference as separate. Do not impute measured casein DH from 'NA'. Primary qualitative null AND all numerical Farup AUCs appeared in search before executing comparisons; no test here was blind. Report method/product/assay/dose differences with Power: hydrolysis methods/source differences preclude assuming a universal coefficient.
