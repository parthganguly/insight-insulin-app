# Direct two-dose test of a second digestibility distinction

After the paired-disaccharide clock and mixed-food protocols were frozen, a primary source search identified PMC11356256: trials of oligomalt and maltodextrin at 33 and/or 50 g, as fasting drinks. Only the products, dose design, clinical-population labels and direction in the title have been seen by the coordinator. The target arm-level numbers have not been inspected. This case could address the missing test of two portions of both versions with directly measured insulin. Establish which participants took which arms; if doses are in separate cohorts that is a transport test rather than within-subject dose escalation. Audit published native 0–120 iAUC operator before deciding coverage. No relabeling of another time window. Do not pool populations or infer a patient-specific response.
