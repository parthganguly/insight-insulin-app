## Design and input

Ten healthy overweight adults, eight men, two women. Mean age 31 (SE 4) years, BMI 27.7 (SE 0.8). Cardiovascular or metabolic disorder and medication exclusions. Randomised single-blind two-period crossover, washout ≥1 week. Cargill funded the study, with one author an advisor. Plasma insulin measured by LINCO human insulin radioimmunoassay.

Morning was fasted 75 g carbohydrate in 400 mL water, consumed in 15 min. Lunch was on that same day and used the corresponding sugar. Total lunch energy was half each person's calculated 24-hour resting energy expenditure. Energy percentages were 55% carbohydrate, 30% fat, 15% protein, **including** 25% of total meal energy as the test sugar beverage. The reported protocol gives no single common lunch sugar gram dose or itemized meal ingredient list. If REE is R kcal/day and a carbohydrate energy factor of 4 kcal/g is adopted, sugar grams = R/32; remaining carbohydrate grams = 0.30×0.5×R/4. This conditional expression provides no observed dose. Morning and lunch contrast different protocols in the same participants, with morning treatment and lunch treatment inseparable.
