# Suraphad et al. 2017, DOI 10.3390/nu9050464, PMID 28481230

[Original MDPI PDF](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/nutrients/nutrients-09-00464/article_deploy/nutrients-09-00464.pdf) and [PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5452194/). HTML/XML and publisher PDF are the same study. PDF SHA256 469f78ec4b99dca41d8921c9a5d5552e0ea7517ed9bd4222c29a400d27ca65c2. The 2017 report recruited 22 people, randomized 18 and analyzed 15 completers (8 women, 7 men). The fifth arm was unsweetened tea. All arms had 400 mL vehicle; the four sweetened arms had 50 g sucrose or isomaltulose, after a 12 h fast; consumption within 5 min. Tea contained an infusion of 4 g leaves at 95°C for five minutes. Single blind, five visits, crossover, two-week washout. Samples at 0,15,30,45,60,90,120 min. Plasma insulin BQ human ELISA. Actual raw fasting insulin levels unavailable; all curves are arm-specific changes from fasting.

Figure 3B, published page 6, reports arm-specific means and SEM for native 2-h trapezoidal iAUC. The negative pure-tea mean excludes positive-only clipping; Figure 2B signed integrated means reproduce the bars closely. No paired difference SEM or participant-level AUC supplied. Figure 3B bar labels a/b/d/b/c (water-sucrose/water-isomaltulose/plain tea/tea-sucrose/tea-isomaltulose); the authors indicate different letters p<.05 via ANOVA and Duncan multiple comparisons. No within-subject ratio uncertainty is identifiable from these marginal SEMs. The two vehicles share participants.

The published relative changes in §3.2 do not agree with all Figure 3B ratios. The text gives 42.0% insulin reduction for the water substitution and a tea-associated reduction of 32.1% with sucrose and 37.7% with isomaltulose. Applying these literally to arm means gives 0.580 in water and 0.532 in tea. The bar ratios are 0.552 and 0.575. The text gives no explicit denominator beyond the comparator or explanation of this difference. Use each reading separately. Both favor the pre-existing donor ratio 0.65477 over equality, with overprediction in each case. The direction of the vehicle difference itself is unresolved. Post-result addition to the original preselection, not two independent test cohorts. Figure 2B and Figure 3B numerical results, 0–60 and 0–120 signed areas, and absolute log errors are in the CSVs.
