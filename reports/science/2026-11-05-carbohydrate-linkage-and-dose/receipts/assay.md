# Can timed glucose release predict the oligomalt substitution?

No same-assay timed substrate-release fractions could be verified for the two intervention carbohydrates. The requested ratios and their model errors are unavailable at each requested time. No parameter was fitted using study outcomes.

| Oligomalt / Glucidex 40 release fraction ratio | unavailable | unavailable | unavailable | unavailable |

Lamothe compared 33 g **total carbohydrate** per arm in 300 mL water (AS15 syrup and Glucidex 40 powder). Its measurements characterize chemical structure and the response in healthy volunteers. Human interstitial glucose iAUC and breath H₂ do not measure mass of glucose produced by hydrolysis of a fixed amount of starting substrate. End-point digestibility cannot determine a cumulative fraction at 20, 60 or 120 minutes. The human glucose 2h-iAUC ratio 116/187 therefore cannot be used as the requested release fraction. The comparator is the named Roquette Glucidex 40, but the original AS15 test material averaged DP 15.6±0.3 with 7.6 g leucrose and 2.2 g fructose per 100 g (Table 1). Glucidex 40 had mean DP 3 with 15.4 g free glucose per 100 g. The free glucose establishes material composition only; use of baseline free glucose in a future assay needs an explicit common definition. [Lamothe original, materials and Tables 1–2](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.carpta.2022.100256)

Johansen tested equal-dose comparisons of 33 g and/or 50 g in 300 mL water, depending on the cohort, and used the same branded DE40 comparator. Its oligomalt averaged DP 16.5±0.1, with 5.3% leucrose, <0.2% fructose and no free glucose. Hence the two publications describe related alternan preparations with different composition. Johansen collected blood glucose, insulin and a four-hour breath H₂ curve in healthy volunteers. H₂ measures colonic fermentation of unabsorbed carbohydrate; it does not quantify a timed release fraction. A nominal DE of 40 is reducing power, which cannot be set to 40% glucose released. [Johansen original, intervention and trial methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11356256/)

The Lamothe manufacturing patent concerned enzymatic synthesis and product distribution; its examples yielded no matched human-digestion assay. The cited mammalian glucosidase study examined isomaltooligosaccharides. Its material identity does not establish the alternating 1,3/1,6 AS15 formulation or the trial's DE40 comparator. An assay on different linkages, a different DP distribution, or maltodextrin DE1 cannot fill this cell. [Product-process patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2021140208A1/en) · [Song et al.
