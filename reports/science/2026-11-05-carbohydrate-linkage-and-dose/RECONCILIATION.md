## Source interpretation and corrections

- **Holub.** Trial 2 used both 50 g drinks, ten healthy adults, fasting, plasma insulin. The methods specify per-person area above fasting. The source reports mean areas of 15,208 and 23,347 pmol·min/L (iso, sucrose) and a three-hour *recording*. The methods omit the integration horizon. The signed group-curve iso area was 15,200 at 120 minutes and 18,497 at 180. Given the same grids and baselines, the average positive area must be at least the signed area. Thus a three-hour assignment conflicts with the figure; proximity to 120 minutes does not prove that horizon. The earlier independent note in this packet explicitly assumed three hours before the discrepancy was resolved to an unknown horizon. The fit uses measured curve coordinates only. [Source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2943747/) · [Independent initial note, superseded on this point](receipts/sol_clock_original.md)
- **van Can 2009.** Baseline was sampled about five minutes before each dose and carried forward to zero. Morning and lunch used linked assignments in the same ten people. A literal −5-to-180 integration nearly reproduces the morning three-hour table; the afternoon table and plot remain discrepant. Neither changes the stipulated 0–120 signed curve calculation. The 75 g morning protocol differs from Holub's 50 g. Absolute areas reported in different assay systems were not harmonized solely for the clock comparison. [Extraction and original tables](receipts/vancan.md)
- **Keesing.** The 12-person biochemical crossover followed a sushi lunch; the 50 g isomaltulose drink included 35 mg sucralose for sweetness. The figure's isomaltulose curve stayed below its pre-drink baseline through 180 minutes. At two hours all transferred positive scalar/clock forecasts of test area are positive on the measured reference curve. Their sign misses this protocol. The prespecified positive-ratio log error is undefined. The authors report iAUC 609 vs 2,492 in their printed units with a paired difference of 1,883 (95% CI 921–2,845). The reported summation window and handling of negative increments are insufficiently explicit to call this participant-positive two-hour evidence. [Original figures and protocol](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu11112647)
- **Suraphad, Suklaew and Zhang.** The Suraphad native trapezoidal bars give ratios of 0.552 in water and 0.575 in green tea; the prose implies 0.580 and 0.532. A negative unsweetened-tea bar rules out positive-only clipping across all arms. Both readings favor the fixed reference over equality. In Suklaew, the tabulated positive first-meal increments support an empirical two-hour ratio; the published iAUC ratio is for four hours. Energy and fat entries conflict but the 40/67 g carbohydrate figures agree. In Zhang the separate sugar-between-meals protocol permits only 60 minutes until another meal. Ratio at 60 minutes is about 0.54 between meals and 0.56 with breakfast. Prior feeding and time of day differ. The published 540-minute total area includes multiple meals. Sol used rounded Zhang concentrations, while the coordinator used unrounded transformations of the saved pixels (two-hour ratios 0.72894 and 0.72907). The difference is smaller than the graph's resolution. [Mixed data audit](receipts/mixed.md) · [Tea source audit](receipts/tea.md)
- **Keller and Kahlhöfer.** The acute drink is 37 g isomaltulose versus 28 g maltodextrin plus 9 g sucrose. The source does not report the target insulin area or time course. Its companion describes the same cohort. The rounded 28% reduction for total AUC gives a separate diagnostic ratio of about 0.72. Multiplying the constituent and linkage adjustments forecasts 0.419, further away than 0.640 from the constituents alone, 0.655 from the linkage alone or equality. [Source and coverage](receipts/keller.md)
- **Johansen and Lamothe.** Manufacturer involvement, distinct clinical populations, three-arm healthy cohort, repeated participants, incomplete periods and missing interaction uncertainty are retained. The breath hydrogen evidence does not establish quantitatively absorbed mass by a given minute. The named Glucidex comparator is shared with Lamothe but the oligomalt composition differs. Timed release on both intervention ingredients could not be established. Glucose iAUC was never substituted for a hydrolysis fraction. [Assay audit](receipts/assay.md) · [Sources and subgroups](receipts/johansen.md)
- **Trehalose.** Sol and Astra traced the reported 36% mean within-person ratio to the Discussion after Opus's initial implementation missed it. The primary ratio is calculated separately from the two reported arm means. Released glucose mass is a hypothetical full-hydrolysis input; minor product constituents and incomplete absorption cannot be filled in from insulin. Four exclusions leave 20 analysed out of 24. The direction of any exclusion bias is unknown. A trapezoid of plotted mean concentrations and positive area integrated for each participant can differ. Measured clinical concentrations also do not establish a mechanistic rate constant. [Independent report](receipts/sol_trehalose.md) · [Conditional native-level calculations](receipts/sol_levels.md)
