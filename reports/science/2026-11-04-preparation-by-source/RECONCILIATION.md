## Source linkage and uncertainty

**Granfeldt.** The original Article Tables 1–2 and Methods link both covered Glacier varieties to their own milled flours. Covered grains were dehulled. Kernels and flours were boiled for 25 and 2.5 minutes with different water ratios. Clinical insulin was measured by serum ELISA at baseline, 30, 45, 95 and 120 minutes; Table 2 calls it plasma. We use the printed 0–120 area and separately II relative to white bread. The paired ratio-of-means for the nine common completers is unavailable. The HI experiment was performed by six chewers with matched barley products, followed by pepsin, pancreatic amylase and dialysis. Reporting HI over 180 minutes supplies no early-time figure. Table 2 reports 75 and 56 for normal and high-amylose flour; the discussion rounds these to about 80 and 60. We used the table. A low-resolution OCR superscript is not a source of pairwise covariance. [Original paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/ajcn/59.5.1075) · [Author-deposited version](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/15027355_)

**Bornet.** A fresh copy of the original HAL scan yielded Table 1 amylose, Table 3 meal duration, Figure 2 clinical trajectories, Figure 3 preparation-linked digestion markers, Figure 4's independent H30 abscissae and Table 5's 0–180 incremental insulin means. The table does not establish positive-only clipping. The graphs are incremental arm means sampled at 30-minute intervals. The coordinate record stores neither individual curves nor individual outcomes. Two independent readings handle the tilt of the scanned axes slightly differently. The independent 120-minute means differ from the coordinator's by up to 4%; the two 180-minute reconstructions use the same exact printed means. For H30 the coordinator traced Figure 3 by line/marker identity. Sol used Figure 4's abscissae, checking the two similarly sized insulin points against Figure 3's product curves. The two digitizations should not be averaged to manufacture extra precision. Mung-bean noodles and smooth peas are distinct conditions in the dataset. Raw cassava is near assay resolution, and small changes in reading matter more for ratios there. The assumed coordinate envelopes and reported intra-assay reproducibility are different quantities. [Original scan](https://hal.inrae.fr/hal-02728571v1/file/1989_Colonna_Am.J.Clin.Nutr_1.pdf)

The distinct 1982 rice study publishes a two-hour group comparison but we could not verify its plotted bar heights. The 2000 oat/barley flake lead measures gelatinization rather than a matched early-hydrolysis series. Those studies are not scored as failed assays or equal insulin outcomes. The adequate new numerical comparisons are barley and the isolated-starch preparation series.
