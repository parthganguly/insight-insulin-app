# Matched early digestion when source and heating both matter

This extension was selected from original article design: nine equal 35 g starch loads, three independently assigned groups of six, each comparing three products within a single processing category. Matched preparations of isolated starch were assayed with porcine pancreatic alpha-amylase. Raw suspensions and 20-minute cooked/cooled gels use wheat, manihot/cassava, and smooth pea. Industrial products use extruded wheat, tapioca and mung-bean noodles: the third source is DIFFERENT. Do not identify a pure industrial-processing effect by equating the two legumes. Meals vary in ingestion time. We have seen the published correlation, qualitative raw manihot result and area/order description, starch composition and breakdown by group; the coordinator has not inspected the numeric insulin outcomes, assay figure coordinates or scored forecasts. The case is not blind validation.

Within each group, the two non-wheat / wheat ratios are primary (cassava/wheat, pea/wheat in raw and gel, tapioca/extruded wheat and noodle/extruded wheat in industrial). Report geometric mean-independent evaluation as mean absolute log-ratio error within the group. Also retain the non-wheat legume/cassava contrast. Score each group separately, without fitting its outcomes; shared reference and repeated subjects mean comparisons are dependent. Target a 0–120 mean-curve signed incremental reconstruction when aggregate graph points are legible, and report exact tabulated 0–180 incremental insulin areas as a separate outcome with its actual conventions. No participant-positive reconstruction from mean curves. Use the published fasting/grid convention. No pooling of cohorts. Record H30 as early hydrolysis from matched cooked/uncooked materials in the accompanying assay, distinct from hummus 20-minute inputs and the barley HI180. If no H20 can be independently secured, report it unavailable in primary branch rather than equating times. The source explicitly reports 30 min reproducibility of 15%; not an exact error interval for any ratio.

Candidates: equal starch and common form within raw/gels; inexpensive amylopectin per served dose using Table 1 amylose percentages (wheat 27%, manioc 17%, smooth pea 35%, mung 32%). Neither is a precise gelatinization model. Use measured H30 ratio, the earlier externally calibrated power and affine functions in PLAN.md; fixed orientation avoids affine nonreciprocity. Use actual percentages and matched serving grams, no target level for primary forecasts. As a diagnostic for heating, evaluate the ratio-of-ratios for cassava versus wheat in gels versus raw. An invariant source factor multiplying a common heat factor predicts 1, as does fixed composition. Paired within-group uncertainty for a population interaction unknown; independent groups for raw/gels. A prior human response learned in raw may be transported to gels separately as a measured-insulin reference, never as food-input prediction. Interpreting starch percent in a 35g loading: the paper says 35g starch dry basis; if it instead gives 35g uncorrected dry product, display purity adjustment sensitivity rather than substituting an unknown dose. Independent verification/digitization and reading variation required. No changing primary comparator or choosing a timepoint to reward a winner.
