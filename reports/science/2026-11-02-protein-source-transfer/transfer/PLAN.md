# Does recording the protein source improve transfer between publications?

Frozen before reading the full comparison rows of Wolever et al. (2024) supplementary file FP in subjects without diabetes, mmc5.pdf, p. 3. The paper's broad associations, the plot heading, first three beef rows, overall estimate, and multiple earlier study outcomes were already exposed. Development and retrospective grouped evaluation. The review searched through July 2023. This is a test of reported aggregate dose and source information under between-study variation. Study rows come from the review authors' own extraction and ratio estimation.

Population without diabetes. Use protein-plus-carbohydrate / matched carbohydrate-control insulin-area ratio as tabulated. Two panels, **reported** 120-minute AUC primary and **calculated** 120-minute AUC secondary. Fit and evaluate separately. Do not infer positive-area equivalence or use these ratios as absolute predictions. Retain the complete selected forest plot rows in source parsing for exclusion and coverage checks. If a panel has fewer than three distinct first authors it is unavailable.

Withhold all rows from the same printed first author for a prediction. Use each remaining author with total weight one (each of that author's rows gets equal weight). Inputs are protein dose / carbohydrate grams, literal protein label (collapse case/space only), first author, method and duration. No inference about undisclosed parts of a meal. On training rows, predict log ratio with (1) null log ratio zero (2) weighted mean (3) ordinary weighted linear regression of log ratio on dose, WITH intercept. If dose range is zero, use weighted mean. (4) same linear prediction plus a shrunken source-specific mean residual. For that source, average residuals within author then across contributing authors, including one additional zero-residual pseudo-author; absent label falls back to (3). This fixes shrinkage before evaluation. Published outcome labels of the held-out author are unavailable to every fit. A dose slope can be an empirical local comparator without being an extrapolatable mechanistic law.

Primary loss: mean across authors of within-author mean absolute log-ratio error; report each model and paired source-minus-dose-only difference. Print author-level improvements, ties and impairments, coverage when the label has independent training support, and loss separately where predictions differ. Separate attributed incremental value of source from the combined candidate beating the no-change/mean. Success for this candidate on the primary metric requires strictly beating both simple references and dose-only on the full primary panel. Preserve secondary results separately. Print leave-one-author-out changes in the summary of paired errors (descriptive influence, not independent confidence intervals) and range extrapolations. These group folds cannot establish independent institutions/cohorts or paired population error. The inexpensive comparator omits more detailed processing and matrix factors. No tuning to this grid. No study outcomes will be inferred from significance alone.
