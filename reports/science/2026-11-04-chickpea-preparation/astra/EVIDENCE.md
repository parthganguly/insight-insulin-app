# Chickpea processing contrasts

This folder contains aggregate evidence from two randomized crossover trials. The same investigators contributed to both studies. They are distinct experiments in different foods, with no evidence here of independent-laboratory replication. Numerical estimates apply to these servings and these populations.

## Population and process

Alshaalan recruited 15 healthy adults. A 240-g intact hummus serving used previously cooked canned whole Kabuli chickpeas in a kitchen blender. A 423-g ruptured serving used fine dry-milled chickpea flour (mean size below 150 µm) boiled for five minutes then blended. Both supplied 26.8 g starch. Fat was 29.5 versus 30.1 g, energy was 460 versus 510 kcal and total water served with the meals was equalized. Changes extend beyond cellular integrity.

Cai recruited 10 healthy adults, feeding a cross-over of three porridges, each supplying 29.5 g starch, 2.3 g sugar, 6.6 g dietary fibre, 11 g protein and 3.7 g fat. The broken-cell arm used high-speed blending while still uncooked, then 28 min at 95°C. Separated intact cells followed 80 min of gentle stirring at 95°C. Cell clusters followed coarse 1.6–2.0 mm milling and 50 min of cooking at 95°C. The different water incorporated during cooking was offset with drinking water. Time, water uptake, particle size and cell intactness can co-vary.

## Endpoints

The fixed-window comparison integrates the published arm-mean insulin concentrations using a trapezoid after subtracting each arm's fasting mean. It represents signed incremental area. Each trial includes 120 and 180 minutes. Linear integration of complete-case arm means supports this summary but does not supply within-person covariance or positive incremental area. Cai sampled at 45 and 150 min, which Alshaalan did not. The hummus graph-labelled iAUC units omit time, methods do not specify the computation, and plotted magnitudes differ markedly from the independently integrated curves. Keep it as a separate author-reported sensitivity. Cai's Figure 2f iAUC bars and Supplementary Table 4 first-peak geometric means refer to different calculations.

The assay endpoints represent gram-equivalents of starch hydrolysed by pancreatic amylase at the indicated time per served portion, from a measured mean percentage multiplied by the starch dose. Neither trial establishes equivalence to Englyst RDS. The 20, 60 and 90 min measurement times are shared. Cai also reports a 120-min assay. Each value uses the observed marker rather than the fitted curve.

The process comparison treats fine milling or intensive blending before heating as broken and both intact conditions as equal. Coarse milling does not by itself establish cellular rupture. The broader hummus labelled food identity and the matched starch dose each predict a tie. The figure reader also calculated a 180-minute sensitivity. The primary frozen comparison uses the 120-minute signed hummus ratio, as specified in ../pilot/PLAN.md. The figure-only extraction is separate from the subsequent public statistical-source reduction. Qualitative results encountered in public search prevent any claim this is fully blinded. The 2025 target and the two linked contrasts cannot estimate generalization error or justify a product coefficient.
