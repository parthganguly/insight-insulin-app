# Claessens chapter 4 insulin curves

The input records 35 plotted group means from figures 4.2B and 4.4B (printed thesis pp. 62 and 64, PDF pages 63 and 65). The publisher figures 2B and 4B show the same arm symbols, time axis, concentration axis and curve shapes. The two versions represent one experiment. Cross-version verification was visual. Raw figures and the PDF are kept outside this folder.

The insulin axes give absolute concentration in µU/mL. The thesis methods specify a fasting sample at time zero, then samples at 15, 30, 60, 90, 120 and 150 minutes after finishing the first drink, which took at most five minutes. Subjects consumed the second drink shortly after the 30-minute sample. Nominal delay is 30 minutes. The experiment supplied no 45-minute sample. The published endpoint is area above baseline minus area below baseline over 150 minutes. Subtract each arm's own time-zero mean before integrating its plotted curve. For a common sampling grid and common participating subjects signed trapezoidal areas commute with averaging. Positive-only areas require individual curves. The tables supply the native 150-minute endpoints. No individual curves, paired covariance or individual positive areas are available here.

Run python extract.py PATH_TO_THESIS_PDF (PyMuPDF) to emit the portable graph input. PDF page coordinates in points originate at the top left. The charts are rotated on the page. Decreasing x means increasing insulin and decreasing y means later study time. The series dictionaries give zero-based drawing indices from Page.get_drawings, marker bounding-box centers, and absolute mean concentrations. Calibrate linearly using the insulin axis positions for 0 and 80 µU/mL. The time coordinate checks the assigned sample to within one minute. Legend symbols for soy and CHO singles are filled square and open diamond. For soy first, CHO first and pea first they are open square, filled diamond and open circle.

Stored decimals preserve deterministic replay. Plot geometry limits accuracy. The source vector paths snap to grid increments around 0.5 PDF points and symbol-center and connecting-line alignments can differ by several tenths of a point. A per-point ±0.5 µU/mL perturbation is a sensitivity assumption. Under this assumption, the worst signed-AUC change for one arm is ±(H−7.5) µU·min/mL at H=90, 120 or 150 (absolute trapezoidal weights after baseline subtraction). This covers geometric sensitivity alone. Five reconstructed 150-minute AUCs differ from the published values by +41.4, +26.1, +4.8, −16.1 and +18.5 µU·min/mL in order soy, CHO, soy first, CHO first, pea first. The largest relative difference is 3.3%. Calibration used the axes alone.

Run python evaluate.py to obtain curve areas, table discrepancies and both order errors. The 150-minute scheduled forecast uses the published full first response plus the graph-cropped second response to 120 minutes. At 120 all terms are graph-derived, with the second response cropped to 90. Controls are the two full-window single responses summed and the first only. Every error is predicted minus observed. The order contrast is CHO first minus soy first. This is an aggregate, within-experiment measured-component benchmark. The primary 150-minute observed table contrast was nonsignificant (paired p=0.263). Pea has a combination arm but lacks a single-component arm.

Run python sampling_grid.py for the post hoc sensitivity. The shifted second component is zero through minute 30 and evaluated at its 30, 60, 90 and 120 minutes at nominal combination minutes 60, 90, 120 and 150. Omitting combination minute 45 removes the single-drink minute-15 rise. The predicted AUC falls by 187.84 when CHO is second and 58.35 when soy is second. This explains why the predicted contrast changes sign. At 150 minutes the script reports both a table-based full first response and observation (primary_source), and all-graph sensitivity. At 120 the two definitions coincide. Sampling explains a difference between model summaries and cannot establish a physiological order effect.

Run python -m unittest -v for replay checks. Figures and tables are aggregate research data.
